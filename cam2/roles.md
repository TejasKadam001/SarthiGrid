# User Roles & Personas - आरोग्य संपन्न वारी

This document outlines the user roles and personas who interact with the **आरोग्य संपन्न वारी** (Healthy Wari) platform. It defines their workflows, use cases, and how the app assists them during the pilgrimage.

---

## 1. Pilgrims / Warkari (वारकरी - The Core User)
*   **Persona:** General pilgrims walking the route under the Dnyaneshwar or Tukaram Maharaj Palkhis. They may have limited tech literacy and are often walking in remote, crowded areas.
*   **Key Needs:** Finding water, toilets, medical relief, and emergency assistance quickly.
*   **App Workflows & Features Used:**
    *   **Locate Me (📍 माझे स्थान):** Instantly centers the map on their location to see help nearby.
    *   **Help Categories Grid:** Quick icons to filter for Toilets, Water, resting spots (Mukkam/Visava), and Doctors.
    *   **Nearest List & Walk Estimation:** Sees how many meters away a facility is and how many minutes of walking it takes.
    *   **Emergency Speed Dials (108 & 112):** Immediate quick-dial for critical health or security needs.
    *   **Help Request Modal:** Submits location and Dindi details to request medical/ambulance help.

---

## 2. Police & Traffic Police (पोलीस व ट्रॅफिक पोलीस)
*   **Persona:** On-duty law enforcement officers deployed at checkpoints, ghat sections, crowd-control blockades, and traffic diversion points along the Palkhi routes.
*   **Key Needs:** Quick coordination, reporting incidents, directing pilgrims, managing traffic blocks, and securing medical access for emergencies.
*   **App Workflows & Features Used:**
    *   **Police Station & Officer Directory:** Access to phone numbers of on-duty officers, inspectors, and police station help desks.
    *   **Map Checkpoints:** Finding nearby police help booths on the map to coordinate blockades and crowd control.
    *   **Ambulance Routing Support:** Identifying closest ambulance points (such as ALS or BLS vehicles) to clear paths for emergency medical transport through crowded streets.

---

## 3. District Administration & Admin Officers (जिल्हा व तालुका प्रशासन)
*   **Persona:** District collectors, Sub-Divisional Officers (SDOs), Tehsildars, and Block Development Officers (BDOs) overseeing the execution and facilities of the Wari pilgrimage in Pune, Satara, and Solapur.
*   **Key Needs:** Overall monitoring, addressing resource shortages (water tanker refills, sanitation complaints), and managing government coordination.
*   **App Workflows & Features Used:**
    *   **District-wise Officer Directory:** Real-time phone lists to quickly call block officers (BDOs), taluka health officers, and control rooms.
    *   **Water Station Mapping:** Monitoring government water tanker filling sites and approximate distribution stations to manage supply chain logistics.
    *   **Mukkam Management:** Using the Mukkam strip to review stops and ensure adequate toilet blocks and shelters are ready at each halt.

---

## 4. Doctors & Medical Staff (वैद्यकीय अधिकारी व आरोग्य कर्मचारी)
*   **Persona:** Medical officers (MOs), nurses, Emergency Medical Technicians (EMTs), and paramedics stationed in ambulances (108/102) or Primary Health Centers (PHCs) along the route.
*   **Key Needs:** Managing emergency referrals, coordinating patient transport, and communicating with ICU/Trauma centers.
*   **App Workflows & Features Used:**
    *   **PHC / Hospital Sub-filters:** Filtering the map to show only Primary Health Centers (PHC), Rural/Sub-district hospitals, or ICU/Trauma centers for patient redirection.
    *   **Ambulance Supplement Details:** Accessing directories of other ambulance crews to transfer patients or request backup.
    *   **Weather Updates:** Preparing medical supplies (e.g., ORS, monsoon medical kits) based on local rainfall predictions.

---

## 5. Volunteers & Charanseva Providers (चरणसेवा स्वयंसेवक व स्वयंसेवी संस्था)
*   **Persona:** Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs), spiritual volunteers, massagers, and local citizens setting up stalls to serve food, shelter, and medical support.
*   **Key Needs:** Distributing resources where needed and getting visibility for their support camps.
*   **App Workflows & Features Used:**
    *   **Charanseva Point Filters:** Directing pilgrims looking for rest, massages, and voluntary services to their specific points.
    *   **Visava Spot Mapping:** Helping pilgrims locate visavas (resting areas) during long daytime walks.
